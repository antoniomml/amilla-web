About Me

I’m a Cloud Security Engineer focused on protecting AWS environments, with experience in Azure and GCP. I enjoy turning security problems into tools and automated processes that make everyday work simpler.

Outside of work, I’m usually busy with one project or another. I have a small homelab at home, and I’m into home automation, 3D printing, FPV drones and, more recently, experimenting with artificial intelligence. I also play a lot of video games and have a soft spot for gadgets I probably don’t need.

But it’s not all about computers. I enjoy analogue photography, travelling and music. I play guitar and sing backing vocals with Lou Cornago, so every now and then I swap AWS for stages, cables and soundchecks.

I’m from Jaén, although I’ve been living in Madrid for a few years now. I studied Computer Engineering and have worked at companies such as Ryanair and Openbank.

This website doesn’t have any grand ambitions. I just wanted a little corner of the Internet where I could share who I am and bring together all the places where you can find me.

If you’d like to know more about my work, you can find me on LinkedIn and GitHub.
